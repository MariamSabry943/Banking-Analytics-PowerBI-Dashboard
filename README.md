# 🏛️ Banking Analytics Power BI Dashboard

An end-to-end business intelligence project designed to transform raw banking data into actionable executive insights. 

## 🚀 Project Overview
This project covers a complete data analysis lifecycle, from initial UI/UX wireframing in Figma to ETL processing, relational data modeling, and advanced DAX measure calculations in Power BI.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages & Features
- **Executive Overview:** High-level KPIs tracking customer volume, total transactions, loan amounts, and overall account balances with dynamic filtering.
- **Customer Insights:** Deep dive into active vs. dormant accounts, account type distributions, and top customer transaction rankings.
- **Transactions:** Comprehensive analysis of transaction types (Payment, Transfer, Deposit, Withdrawal) and seasonal trends.
- **Loans & Details:** Portfolio breakdown by loan status, types, interest rates, and a dedicated drill-through details view for individual loans.
- **Support & Satisfaction:** Tracking customer support calls, resolution rates, and issue categories across regions.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
- **UI/UX Design:** Figma (Dark mode theme, custom sidebar navigation, icons, and layout mockups)
- **Data Transformation & ETL:** Power Query
- **Data Modeling:** Star Schema architecture (Relational mapping between Customers, Loans, and Transactions)
- **Calculations:** Advanced DAX Measures
- **Visualization:** Power BI Desktop

## 🔍 Key Insights

* High volume of unresolved customer support issues reaching **52.66%**, indicating operational bottlenecks in resolution times.
* **Army State** records the highest concentration of unresolved customer issues compared to other states.
* **Business accounts** act as the primary revenue drivers, generating the highest transaction volume and total balance.
* Most active customers hold between **4 to 7 active accounts**.
* The year **2020** marked the peak customer acquisition milestone with **551 new customers**.
* A distinct segment of registered users exists who currently maintain no active banking accounts.

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

* **Optimize Customer Support Infrastructure:** Restructure the support workflow in **Army State** and implement automated ticketing systems or FAQs to drastically reduce the 52.66% unresolved issue rate.
* **Focus on Business Account Growth:** Capitalize on the high-value business sector by offering tailored financial products, lower transaction fees, and dedicated corporate support tiers.
* **Re-engage Accountless Leads:** Deploy targeted email marketing campaigns and onboarding incentives to convert registered users without accounts into active banking participants.
* **Analyze Historical Acquisition Success:** Review the marketing and operational strategies deployed during the 2020 peak period to replicate successful customer acquisition channels.
